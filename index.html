Quick Energy Wisdom

  • Ceiling Fans: AC fans use ~75W; BLDC inverter fans consume only ~30W (saving 60% battery energy).
  • Inverter ACs: Once the room cools down, an inverter AC stabilizes between 400W–600W rather than maximum rated draw.
  • Lithium vs Tubular: Tubular lead-acid batteries should only be drained to 50% DoD to preserve 3–5 year lifespan.
